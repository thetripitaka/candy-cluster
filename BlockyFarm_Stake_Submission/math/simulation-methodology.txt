SIMULATION METHODOLOGY
=====================

Game Title: Blocky Farm
Provider: 8-bit Wizardry
Version: 1.0.0

PURPOSE
-------
Simulations were conducted to validate the theoretical RTP,
volatility profile, feature contribution, and maximum win behavior
of Blocky Farm.

SIMULATION APPROACH
-------------------
- Monte Carlo simulation methodology was used
- Simulations were executed over a large number of spins
  (millions of iterations) to ensure statistical reliability
- RNG behavior matches the production game logic

WHAT WAS INCLUDED
-----------------
The simulations fully accounted for:
- Base game cluster pays
- Tumbling (cascading) win mechanics
- Global multiplier progression
- Free Spins feature
- Retrigger mechanics during Free Spins
- Second Chance Wild feature
- Boosted Wild cluster payouts
- Infused Scatter multiplier advancement
- Maximum win cap enforcement

WHAT WAS EXCLUDED
-----------------
- No external modifiers or promotional mechanics
- No manual intervention or scripted outcomes
- No player behavior assumptions

RTP VALIDATION
--------------
- RTP was calculated as total winnings divided by total bets
- Feature contributions were included in the final RTP figure
- Observed RTP converged toward the theoretical value
  as simulation size increased

VOLATILITY ANALYSIS
-------------------
- Win frequency and win size distribution were analyzed
- Base game provides frequent lower-value wins
- Larger wins occur less frequently and are driven by:
  - Extended tumble sequences
  - Higher global multipliers
  - Free Spins sessions

MAXIMUM WIN ANALYSIS
-------------------
- Maximum achievable win is capped at 10,000× bet
- Simulations confirmed the cap is enforced correctly
- Extreme win outcomes occur rarely and align with
  the intended volatility profile

CONCLUSION
----------
Simulation results support the stated RTP, volatility classification,
and feature behavior of Blocky Farm.
All outcomes are consistent with the design intent
and long-term statistical expectations.
