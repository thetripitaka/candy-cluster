MATH OVERVIEW
=============

Game Title: Blocky Farm
Provider: 8-bit Wizardry
Version: 1.0.0

GAME STRUCTURE
--------------
- Slot type: Cluster Pays
- Grid size: 6×5
- Minimum cluster size: 5 symbols
- Winning symbols are removed and replaced via tumbling mechanics

PAYOUT MECHANICS
----------------
- Wins are calculated based on cluster size and symbol paytables
- Multiple wins may occur within a single spin due to cascading symbols
- All wins are multiplied by an active global multiplier

GLOBAL MULTIPLIER
-----------------
- Multiplier starts at 1× on each base game spin
- Multiplier increases with every winning tumble
- Progression path:
  1× → 2× → 3× → 5× → 8× → 12× → 20×
- Multiplier resets when no further winning clusters are formed
- During Free Spins, the multiplier does not reset

FEATURES
--------
Wild Symbol:
- Substitutes for all regular symbols
- Does not substitute for Scatter symbols

Scatter Symbol:
- Triggers Free Spins when 3 or more appear
- Base Game: 3+ Scatters award 10 Free Spins
- Free Spins: 3+ Scatters award 5 additional Free Spins

Second Chance Wild:
- If no further wins are available after a tumble sequence,
  a Wild symbol may randomly appear on the grid
- This provides a final opportunity for an additional win

Boosted Wild Clusters:
- Winning clusters containing 2 or more Wild symbols
  receive a 1.6× boost to their base payout
- Boost is applied before the global multiplier

Infused Scatter Mechanic:
- If exactly 2 Scatter symbols are present during a winning tumble,
  the global multiplier temporarily advances by one additional step
- This effect may occur once per spin

RTP AND VOLATILITY
-----------------
- Theoretical RTP: 97.00%
- Volatility classification: Medium–High
- Maximum win: 10,000× bet

GENERAL NOTES
-------------
- All calculations are deterministic based on RNG outcomes
- No adaptive or player-influenced RTP logic is present
- All features are included in the RTP calculation
