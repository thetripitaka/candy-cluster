VOLATILITY SUMMARY
==================

Game Title: Blocky Farm
Provider: 8-bit Wizardry
Version: 1.0.0

VOLATILITY PROFILE
------------------
Blocky Farm is classified as a:

- Medium–High Volatility Slot

This classification reflects the balance between:
- Frequency of winning spins
- Average win size
- Potential for larger wins via multipliers and Free Spins

VOLATILITY CHARACTERISTICS
--------------------------
- Base game provides frequent small-to-medium wins through cluster pays
- Tumbling mechanics allow multiple wins within a single spin
- A global multiplier increases with each successive tumble
- Larger wins are less frequent but significantly amplified by multipliers
- Free Spins feature increases overall win potential and volatility

WIN DISTRIBUTION
----------------
- Majority of wins occur in the base game
- High-value wins are typically achieved through:
  - Extended tumble sequences
  - Advanced global multipliers
  - Free Spins with persistent multiplier progression

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
-----------------
- Regular engagement through frequent cascades
- Occasional high-impact win moments
- Suitable for players who enjoy momentum-based gameplay
  with the potential for larger payouts

DISCLAIMER
----------
Volatility classification is based on statistical analysis
and observed win distribution across simulations.
Short-term gameplay results may vary.
