GAME TITLE
Blocky Farm

GAME TYPE
Cluster Pays Slot
6×5 grid with tumbling (cascading) wins

RTP
Theoretical RTP: 97.0%

VOLATILITY
High

MAX WIN
10,000× bet

GAMEPLAY OVERVIEW
Blocky Farm is a 6×5 cluster slot game where wins are formed by landing
5 or more identical symbols connected anywhere on the grid.

Winning symbols are removed and new symbols tumble into place.
This process continues until no further winning clusters are formed.

MULTIPLIER MECHANIC
- A global multiplier starts at 1× on each spin
- The multiplier increases with every winning tumble
- Multiplier progression:
  1× → 2× → 3× → 5× → 8× → 12× → 20×
- The multiplier resets when no further winning tumbles occur

FREE SPINS
- Free Spins are triggered by landing 3 or more Scatter symbols
- Base Game:
  - 3 or more Scatters award 10 Free Spins
- During Free Spins:
  - 3 or more Scatters award 5 additional Free Spins
- The global multiplier does not reset during Free Spins
- Multiplier progression persists for the entire Free Spins session

SPECIAL FEATURES

Wild Symbol:
- Substitutes for all regular symbols
- Does not substitute for Scatter symbols

Second Chance Wild:
- After a spin finishes tumbling with no remaining wins,
  a Wild symbol may randomly appear on the grid
- This provides a final chance to create an additional cluster win

Boosted Wild Clusters:
- If a winning cluster contains 2 or more Wild symbols,
  that cluster’s payout is boosted by 1.6×
- The boost is applied before the global multiplier

Infused Scatters:
- If exactly 2 Scatter symbols are present during a winning tumble:
  - The global multiplier temporarily advances by one additional step
  - This effect can occur once per spin

GENERAL INFORMATION
- All wins are paid according to the paytable and active multipliers
- Maximum win is capped at 10,000× the bet
- Malfunction voids all pays and plays
- Incomplete or interrupted games may be resumed
